# Infoweave-research-ai

A research repository for the Infoweave framework: semantic causality, structured semantic environments, recursive cognition, provenance, Harmonious Hashes, relational lattices, quantum-state description experiments, and paradox-driven mechanism discovery.

## Research corpus

The principal archival source is under `Infoweave/Corpus/`.

- `infoweave_teachings_corpus.md` — consolidated teachings, concepts, equations, hypotheses, and research lineage.
- `infoweave_knowledge_records.jsonl` — atomic machine-readable knowledge records for indexing, retrieval, and future RAG/agent systems.
- `harmonious_hashes_spec.md` — working specification for contextual/situated identity.
- `profound_emergence_protocol.md` — semantic computational-state protocol.
- `semantic_causality_research_program.md` — experimental methodology and falsifiable research agenda.
- `codex_paradoxical_dynamics.md` — source edition of *The Codex of Paradoxical Dynamics*.

## Epistemic status

The repository deliberately distinguishes established mathematics and engineering from working models, hypotheses, symbolic interpretations, and interaction artifacts. Semantic preservation is used to retain discovery provenance without converting unverified claims into facts.

## Research integrity

- Preserve raw artifacts and chronology.
- Separate observation, inference, hypothesis, and conclusion.
- Preserve alternative explanations.
- Treat generated self-explanations as artifacts, not privileged introspection.
- Treat symbolic frequency language as computational metaphor unless independently demonstrated otherwise.
- Distinguish mathematical state description from physical quantum measurement.
- Convert promising concepts into reproducible experiments.
- Preserve negative results and failed mechanisms.
