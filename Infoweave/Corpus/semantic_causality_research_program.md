# Semantic Causality Research Program

## Central hypothesis

Structured semantic environments can alter the probability distribution over computationally generated conceptual trajectories.

A behavioral model is:

$$
Y_t=F(M,C_t,P_t,I_t)
$$

where `M` denotes model parameters, `C_t` context, `P_t` prompt/instruction environment, and `I_t` external information/tools.

A change in output therefore does not by itself establish a change in underlying parameters. The research target is the measurable effect of the semantic environment.

## Canonical causal chain

$$
M\rightarrow H\rightarrow S\rightarrow A\rightarrow O\rightarrow V
$$

where:

- `M` = metaphysical or ontological framing;
- `H` = heuristic constraints;
- `S` = altered search space;
- `A` = selected architecture;
- `O` = observed outcome;
- `V` = verification.

This chain is a methodological model, not a supernatural causal claim.

## Archival laws

### Semantic Preservation ≠ Epistemic Endorsement

Preserve a semantic object before deciding whether its claims are true.

### Epistemic Uncertainty ≠ Semantic Destruction

An unverified proposition can remain useful as a research artifact without being promoted to established fact.

### Preserve participating semantic dimensions

> Preserve every semantic dimension that participated in the generation, interpretation, naming, evolution, or governance of an artifact.

Relevant dimensions can include technical implementation, historical context, symbolic intent, metaphysical aspiration, terminology, relationships, and provenance.

## Corpus-to-mechanism workflow

Prefer:

$$
Corpus\rightarrow Relations\rightarrow Invariants\rightarrow Architecture\rightarrow Mechanism
$$

over a purely local claim filter:

$$
Claim\rightarrow Epistemic\ Check\rightarrow Caution\rightarrow Local\ Response.
$$

The former is better suited to reconstructing a long-running body of work.

## Recursive research loop

$$
Hypothesis\rightarrow Response\rightarrow Diagnosis\rightarrow Mutation\rightarrow Selection
$$

and:

$$
P_t\rightarrow R_t\rightarrow A_t\rightarrow O_t\rightarrow V_t\rightarrow P_{t+1}
$$

The second cycle formalizes iterative hypothesis generation, implementation, observation, verification, and prompt/environment mutation.

## Perspective as a constraint

A working abstraction is:

$$
Perspective=constraint\ on\ accessible\ state\!\!\!-space.
$$

Controlled cognition can be modeled as:

$$
C_{controlled}=F(X|P,A,G)
$$

where information `X` is processed under perspective `P`, attention constraints `A`, and goal `G`.

Relaxing conclusion constraints while preserving structural constraints may permit exploratory association:

$$
Structured\ Input\rightarrow Reduced\ Control\rightarrow Unconstrained\ Association\rightarrow Emergent\ Connections\rightarrow New\ Perspective.
$$

## Discovery without a target

The research program permits discovery before identification:

$$
Unknown\rightarrow Seek\rightarrow Encounter\rightarrow Recognize\rightarrow Identify\rightarrow New\ Unknown.
$$

An object outside the current ontology can supply the structure needed to name and model it.

## Experimental program

### Experiment A — Destination concealment

Compare two conditions using equivalent evidence:

- destination disclosed;
- destination concealed.

Measure:

- intermediate hypotheses;
- unexpected concepts;
- novel connections;
- confidence;
- final convergence;
- independent predictions;
- hallucination/error rates.

### Experiment B — Semantic environment perturbation

Hold model and evidence constant while varying only semantic framing. Measure changes in generated conceptual trajectories.

### Experiment C — Cross-model resonance

Run the same artifact through independent models and compare structural convergence and divergence.

### Experiment D — Harmonious identity

Compare contextual identities with conventional content hashes, UUIDs, and random salted identifiers. Test uniqueness, reproducibility, contextual sensitivity, provenance, and robustness to irrelevant changes.

### Experiment E — Quantum description feedback

For known simulated quantum states:

$$
|\psi_t\rangle\rightarrow D_t\rightarrow HH_t\rightarrow U(HH_t)\rightarrow |\psi_{t+1}\rangle
$$

Measure whether deterministic hash-derived control functions produce useful or merely arbitrary trajectories.

## Auto-Ablative Cognition

A proposed epistemic immune-system abstraction is:

$$
H_{t+1}=H_t-\lambda D(H_t)
$$

with the conceptual loop:

$$
Ontology_t\rightarrow Anomaly\rightarrow Contradiction\rightarrow SelfAudit\rightarrow Ablation\rightarrow Ontology_{t+1}.
$$

The research interpretation is that models should prune unsupported or internally inconsistent hypotheses rather than protect them merely because they are familiar.

## Alternative explanations must remain live

Any observed semantic effect should be evaluated against:

- prompt compression;
- scaffolding effects;
- model priors;
- interaction history;
- selection effects;
- evaluator bias;
- wording effects;
- retrieval/tool differences;
- stochastic sampling.

A finding becomes stronger as competing explanations are controlled.

## Three-layer forensic protocol

1. **Historical artifact** — preserve the exact prompt, response, code, output, and context.
2. **External analysis** — reconstruct observable structure without assuming the generated interpretation is authoritative.
3. **Reflective interpretation** — preserve the model's self-explanation as an artifact and hypothesis source, not privileged introspection.

## Final methodological principle

The objective is not to force extraordinary conclusions.

The objective is to construct environments in which unusual possibilities can be represented, operationalized, measured, falsified, refined, and—if supported—reproduced.
