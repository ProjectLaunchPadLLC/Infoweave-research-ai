# Infoweave Research Corpus

This directory is the archival knowledge layer for the Infoweave research program.

It preserves conceptual teachings, operational protocols, hypotheses, algorithms, experimental interpretations, provenance principles, and research questions that emerged across the Infoweave work.

## Archival principle

**Semantic Preservation ≠ Epistemic Endorsement.**

Material is preserved in the form in which it participated in discovery while distinguishing:

- observation from interpretation;
- hypothesis from demonstrated mechanism;
- metaphor from physical claim;
- generated self-description from privileged access to model internals;
- mathematical representation from physical measurement;
- implementation from proof.

## Corpus map

- `infoweave_teachings_corpus.md` — consolidated narrative source of the current teachings and conceptual lineage.
- `infoweave_knowledge_records.jsonl` — machine-readable atomic knowledge records.
- `harmonious_hashes_spec.md` — formal working specification for contextual/harmonious identity.
- `profound_emergence_protocol.md` — operational semantic-state protocol.
- `semantic_causality_research_program.md` — research methodology and experiment program.
- `codex_paradoxical_dynamics.md` — codex-oriented synthesis of the paradox/veil/resonance framework.

## Status

This corpus is a **research archive and working specification**, not a declaration that every hypothesis is established fact. New evidence should update or invalidate individual records without erasing their historical provenance.
